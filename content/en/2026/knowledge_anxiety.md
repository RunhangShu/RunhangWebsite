Knowledge anxiety people will usually think: there are over 160 million books out there. If I read 30 books per year, I will need over 5 million years to completly reading all of them. Actually, I will never finish reading as there are ~4 million books published each year. 


**How to solve knowledge anxiety - will it help if I glance at one book per subject in my lifetime?**

I've been wanting to search the ocean of the books and find one sentence that can enlighten me. That is a very powerful sentence, striking enough to touch the me. It will be very personal. But don't all of us look for this "aha-moment" when we read for whatever reason? 

In this era of chatbots, I am curious if AI can help me succinctly pick a few books per subject that cover everything in our modern lives. I mean it can be history, religion, world war too, but I am just not particularly interested in that type of book, which is tends to be subjective. I strongly believe history is written by the dominant, powerful winner as losers die and cannot write down their glories. The winner will always glorify their accomplishment while downplaying their mistakes. Because we cannot replay history like a YouTube video, the words cannot be trusted. Do not get me wrong, I still believe the key events are real, it is just the rationale behind these decision are often more complicated than words from a third party can describe. 

What I really cherish are the words that you put evidence on. The fictions that enlighten people to think philosophically. So, if your interests do align with mine, let's try the AI-generated 300-book lists.
